Figmafolio now has an Analytics screen, so you can see who opens your site. It arrived on 27 January 2026 and was tidied up over the next two days.

## What you see

Pick a site, then a period: day, 7 days, 30 days or 12 months. At the top are six numbers:

- Unique visitors
- Total visits
- Pageviews
- Views per visit
- Bounce rate
- Visit duration

Below them are lists of where visitors came from (sources), their countries, their devices, their browsers and their operating systems. Tap a metric to change the chart. The screen also works on a phone.

## Who it is for

Analytics is on the Solo and Pro plans. The trial and Basic plans do not include it.

## What it counts

It counts visits to your Figmafolio site. It does not show which part of your prototype people looked at inside Figma.

Read more on the [analytics feature page](/features/analytics).
