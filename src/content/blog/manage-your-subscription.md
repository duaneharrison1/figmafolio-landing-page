The billing page now shows where your plan stands and lets you change it without contacting me.

## What the page shows

- **On a paid plan:** your plan name and "Next billing date".
- **On a plan you have cancelled:** an "Ending soon" badge and "Active until" with the date. The site stays online until then.
- **After it ends:** a note that your subscription has ended, with a way to choose a plan again.

## Manage Billing and Cancel Subscription

"Manage Billing" opens the billing portal, for your payment and invoices. "Cancel Subscription" takes you to the cancellation page. When you come back, the page checks the result and tells you whether the subscription was cancelled.

## Reactivate

If you cancel and change your mind before the end date, press Reactivate. You are billed normally at your next billing cycle.

Prices differ by country, so they are not listed here. The [pricing page](/pricing) shows the price for where you are, and the [plan comparison](/is-figmafolio-free) shows which plan has what.
