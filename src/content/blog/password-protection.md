Some work is not for everyone: a client project, something under NDA, a portfolio for one recruiter. Password protection, added in June 2024, lets you ask for a password before anyone sees your site.

## How it works

Open your site's settings, find Password, turn on "Enable Password Protection" and type a password of at least 6 characters. Visitors see a password prompt first. To change it later, type a new one. The saved password stays until you do.

## Custom domains

It works on your own domain as well. A few fixes in July 2024 fixed problems with the password on custom domains.

## Plans

Password protection is on Basic, Solo and Pro. In May 2026 I fixed a bug where it did not work for Basic users. See [that update](/blog/password-protection-on-basic).

## One thing to know

The password protects your Figmafolio site, not the Figma prototype behind it. Keep the original Figma link private.
