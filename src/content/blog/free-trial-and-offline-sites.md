The free trial is 7 days. I changed two things about it in November 2026, so the days now match everywhere you see them.

## The 7 days start at your first project

The clock starts when you publish your first project. If you have no project yet, it starts at sign-up. Before, different pages could count from different dates and show different numbers. They now all use the same start date.

While the trial runs, your site shows how long is left, such as "3 days left" or "Last day".

## What happens on day 8

If you have not picked a plan, the site goes offline. A paid plan, or a one-time payment, keeps it online.

- **You** see "Your site is offline" when you open it, with an Upgrade button.
- **Visitors** see a page saying the site is unavailable, so they are not sent to a broken prototype.

Choose a plan and the site is served again.

## Choosing a plan

Pick a plan from the billing page in your account. Basic, Solo and Pro are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Prices differ by country, so the page shows the price for where you are.
