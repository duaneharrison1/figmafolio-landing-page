A favicon is the small icon that shows in the browser tab, the bookmarks bar and the history. Since April 2024 you can upload your own, so your site shows your icon instead of ours.

## How to add one

Open your site's settings, find Favicon, and choose "Upload image". Use a PNG, JPG or SVG at least 70 by 70 pixels. For the best result use 260 by 260 pixels or more. To swap it later, choose "Change image". You can also delete it.

## Where it shows

It shows on your Figmafolio address and on your own domain. In the first weeks after launch I fixed both, so the icon now appears on either.

## Plans

A custom favicon is on Basic, Solo and Pro. The trial does not include it. Basic and above can also remove the "Made with Figmafolio" label, so a paid site can look fully yours.
