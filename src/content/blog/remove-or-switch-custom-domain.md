Switching a site to a different domain used to hide a failure on our side, and the old domain could stay attached with no way for you to release it.

Now:

- There is a **Remove domain** action, with a confirmation, on desktop and on mobile.
- When a domain is connected, the site settings show it with a **Connected** badge and a menu.
- If switching fails, you see the error message, and the new domain is not added until the old one is removed.

The Connected badge shows that the domain is attached to Figmafolio. It does not check your DNS.

See [custom domain](/features/custom-domain).
