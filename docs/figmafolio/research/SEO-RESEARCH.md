# SEO research and page plan (FigmaFolio marketing site)

Step 4 of the SEO programme, run 20 Nov 2026. Baseline numbers are in `BASELINE.md` and the project doc `Figmafolio_Analysis_SEOBaseline_21-11-2026_12-45_63218`. Facts about other tools come from their own pages, read on 20 Nov 2026. Where a page was silent the table says "Not stated".

**Limit of this research:** I have no keyword tool, so there are no monthly search volumes. The plan is ranked by who already ranks, how weak those results are, and how well FigmaFolio fits. Search Console on a figmafolio.com property will give real queries within weeks of it being set up.

## What people are trying to do

| Task they type | What they want | Fit with FigmaFolio |
|---|---|---|
| figma prototype to website | Put a prototype they already made online, with interactions | Strong. This is the product. |
| figma portfolio custom domain | A portfolio at their own address | Strong. Solo and Pro. |
| password protect figma prototype | Share client or NDA work privately | Medium. Figma has its own password for some plans. |
| figma sites alternative | Something other than Figma Sites | Mixed. Most searchers want a full site builder, which FigmaFolio is not. |
| figma portfolio website how to host | Free or cheap hosting for a Figma portfolio | Medium. |
| figma to html | Code out of Figma | None. Framer, Siter and Anima own this. FigmaFolio does not convert to code. |

## Who ranks today

**"figma prototype to website"** (first page, 20 Nov): a run of Figma forum threads, where staff reply that Figma is not a website builder; a Figma web-development page; TeleportHQ, Webflow and Framer guides; Siter and Vev. Duane's own Figma forum post (Jan 2025, about 1,400 views) is on the first page. figmafolio.com is not. No result is an answer-first page for "keep my prototype's interactions and put it online". That is the gap.

**"figma portfolio custom domain":** Figma's own help page and its forum, full of people who cannot connect a domain, including Education plan users. Nothing from a tool that does this for a prototype.

**"password protect figma prototype":** Figma's help article, Figma forum threads, and a Crypto plugin tutorial from Hypermatic. User testing tools (UserTesting, Maze) appear for password-protected prototypes; that is Aero territory, not FigmaFolio.

**"figma sites alternative":** Pixpa (dated 8 Jun 2026), Squarespace, SourceForge, Slashdot, HubSpot-style roundups. Pixpa's list is Pixpa, Framer, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix Studio, WordPress.com, Carrd, Shopify, then Figma Sites. It names no tool that publishes a Figma prototype, and does not mention FigmaFolio, Siter or Detachless.

## Wider set: portfolio builders and quick hosting (partly read, 20 Nov 2026)

Duane named Framer, Siter, GitHub Pages, UXfolio and Readymag, and asked for the whole field of designer portfolio tools and quick page hosting. The searches ran; the pricing pages did not (see the end of this section).

**Who the "best UX portfolio builder" roundups name** (these are the pages a job-seeking designer reads):
- UX Design Institute lists Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy, Webflow, Pixpa, Portfoliobox, UXfolio, Carbonmade, Adobe Portfolio and Krop.
- Uxcel's list of 12 includes UXfolio, described as built specifically for UX designers.
- Designlab names Squarespace as its first recommendation (article dated 20 Feb 2023).
- Colorlib's free-builder list shows Pixpa at $4 a month, Adobe Portfolio free with Creative Cloud including a custom domain, and Cargo with a free plan of 3 pages.
- Pixpa's own roundup says it is updated every quarter, last in June 2026.
- None of these lists names FigmaFolio or any other tool that publishes a live Figma prototype. Roundup pages like these are written by the tools that sell builders, so FigmaFolio is unlikely to be added; the way in is the task pages above.

**What this changes in the plan**
- Add UXfolio and Readymag to Tier 2 as comparison candidates. UXfolio is the closest in audience (UX designers, case studies); Readymag is a design-led builder.
- GitHub Pages, Netlify and Tiiny.host are the "quick hosting" route a designer takes instead. They fit a page 2 or 3 section ("other ways to host a portfolio") rather than their own comparison pages.
- A "best Figma portfolio hosting options" guide may be worth a Tier 3 slot, written fairly, with FigmaFolio as one option and its limits stated.

### New pages, in tiers

**Tier 1: task pages (build first)**

1. **Figma prototype to website** (PICK, first). Answer-first: paste a prototype link and it is a site; interactions and transitions are Figma's; change it in Figma and the site changes. Says plainly what it is not: not real pages, not code. Best gap found: the first page of results has forum threads and no direct answer.
2. **Figma portfolio on your own domain.** Solo and Pro. Compares honestly with Figma Sites on custom domains (Professional plan and up per Figma's help page) and Siter. Links to the pricing page for amounts.
3. **Password protect a Figma prototype.** Starts with how Figma's own password works and who gets it (Professional, Education and up), then where FigmaFolio fits (Basic and up, on your own link, for a site rather than a file). Fair to Figma.
4. **One link for desktop and mobile prototypes.** Smaller, but nothing competes with it.

**Tier 2: comparison pages ("X alternative"), after the admin pages are live**

5. **Figma Sites alternative for free Figma users.** The angle that is true and sourced: Figma Sites needs a paid plan to publish. Says where Figma Sites is better (real pages, CMS).
6. **FigmaFolio vs Siter.io.** Siter makes real pages and is the better pick for that; FigmaFolio keeps prototype interactions. Prices from siter.io/pricing, dated.
7. **FigmaFolio vs Framer.** Not stated pricing yet; read Framer's page on the day.
8. **FigmaFolio vs Carrd.** Carrd is cheaper without Figma; read on the day.

**Tier 3: guides, only if Tier 1 shows impressions**

9. **How to share a Figma prototype so anyone can open it.** Uses the link check FigmaFolio already has. Forum threads show people stuck here.
10. **Why a recruiter may see a warning on your portfolio link.** Duane's insight (13 Nov). Needs real sources first: I have none yet. Do not write from the note alone.

**Not planned:** Figma to HTML or code, user testing (Aero), other languages, "best portfolio sites" roundups.
